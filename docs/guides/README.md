# How to use the app

Step-by-step guides for each part of the app. Finance, Oneline, Script and Onset match the checklist you'll see at the top of that tab.

- **[Home](home.md)** — The Home tab shows where the project stands at a glance — shots, progress, budget spend and hot costs — and brings in the latest from ShotGrid. It also makes the packs the crew open on their phones.
- **[Reports](reports.md)** — The Reports tab makes the show's reports from the last update from ShotGrid: cost workbooks and PDFs, hot costs, vendors and shot lists. Two buttons at the top right of the window open the reports for producers and executives, and Report Studio for designing your own.
- **[Finance](finance.md)** — The Finance tab keeps the show's budget, what has been spent, and what is left. It builds the cost tracker spreadsheet and sends the totals to ShotGrid.
- **[Brief](brief.md)** — The Brief tab makes a work brief from a ShotGrid playlist or sequence: each shot's latest picture, brief, notes and drawings, as a spreadsheet and a PDF.
- **[Upload](upload.md)** — The Upload tab sends a delivery folder to ShotGrid: movies go on their shots (or slates), PO, invoice and bid documents onto their records, and frame sequences are noted but never uploaded. You see where every file goes before anything is sent.
- **[CSV](csv.md)** — The CSV tab turns any ShotGrid page saved as a CSV file into a tidy PDF report. You choose the columns, and each shot's picture can go in too.
- **[Oneline](oneline.md)** — The Oneline tab turns production's oneline schedule into ShotGrid's shoot days, so every day knows its scenes and locations.
- **[Script](script.md)** — The Script tab reads production's draft scripts: a spreadsheet of every scene, the files that bring ShotGrid's Scenes up to date, and a picture of each scene for its thumbnail.
- **[Board](board.md)** — The Board tab builds the unit calendar, the location map and the studio-vs-location calendar from the shoot days at your last sync, as files you can open, print and email. It also turns the post team's calendar exports into a post calendar and ShotGrid's Post Days.
- **[Plan](plan.md)** — The Plan tab is where you write what VFX needs on each coming shoot day and turn it into a ready forward-look email or a PDF. Nothing is ever sent from the app and nothing goes to ShotGrid. You copy the email into your own mail.
- **[Movs](movs.md)** — The Movs tab lists every movie (.mov) file in a folder with the shot each one belongs to, its version and its length, and saves the list as a CSV. The folder is only read.
- **[Onset](onset.md)** — The Onset tab gathers each shoot day's set paperwork and sends the slates and clips to ShotGrid.
- **[Tools](tools.md)** — The Tools tab opens the separate VFX tools that work alongside this app, each in its own window. It only starts them: each tool is installed on the computer on its own.
- **[Settings](settings.md)** — Settings opens from the gear button at the top right of the window (its title bar says Manage Projects). It holds each show's setup: the ShotGrid login, folders, budget figures and how reports look. It also moves a setup between machines and checks for updates.
