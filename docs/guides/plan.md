# Plan: how to

The Plan tab is where you write what VFX needs on each coming shoot day and turn it into a ready forward-look email or a PDF. Nothing is ever sent from the app and nothing goes to ShotGrid. You copy the email into your own mail.

Each job below lists what to press, in order, and what you'll see when it has worked.

**Jobs on this tab**

- [First-time setup](#plan-setup) — Once per show, before the first forward-look email.
- [New crew list issued](#plan-crew) — When the production office sends a new issue of the crew list.
- [Forward look email](#plan-forward) — Each week, ahead of the coming shoot days with VFX.

## First-time setup

*When: Once per show, before the first forward-look email.*

Read the crew list, choose who receives each department's asks, and set the email's wording.

### 1. Read the crew list

Choose the production office's crew list PDF. The app keeps each person's department, job, name and email, and never their phone numbers.

**Press:** `Read a crew list…`

✓ When it has worked: The crew list card says how many people and departments it holds, and how many have an email.

### 2. Choose who gets each department's asks

To start with, each department's asks go to its head from the crew list. Tick anyone else who should get them.

1. Press Open the planner, then Who gets what along the top.
2. Pick a department on the left and tick who receives its asks. Someone with no email on the crew list can't be ticked.
3. Back to the department's heads undoes your choices for that department.
4. Under Always copied in, find someone by name, job or department and press Copy them in. They go on Cc of every email.

**Press:** `Open the planner`

✓ When it has worked: Each department on the left shows how many people get its asks.

### 3. Set the email's wording

On Wording, type the greeting, opening line, section titles, closing line and sign-off, and the list of lidar and reference work you can tick. Every computer that receives this project's setup gets the same wording.

1. Press Wording along the top of the planner.
2. Keep {what} in the opening line. It becomes the days the email is about.
3. Put each kind of reference work on its own line.

✓ When it has worked: The wording is kept as you type. Close the planner when you're done.

## New crew list issued

*When: When the production office sends a new issue of the crew list.*

Read the new issue and check who receives each department's asks.

### 1. Read the new crew list

Choose the new PDF. It replaces the one held. Your choices of who gets each department's asks are kept for everyone whose email hasn't changed.

**Press:** `Read a crew list…`

✓ When it has worked: The status line says who is new, who is no longer listed, and who moved department or changed email.

### 2. Check who gets what

Look over each department whose people changed, so the asks still reach the right people.

1. Press Open the planner, then Who gets what.
2. Check the ticks for each department named in the status line.
3. Anyone on Always copied in who has left shows as no longer on the crew list. Select them and press Take off the list.

**Press:** `Open the planner`

✓ When it has worked: Every department shows the right people.

## Forward look email

*When: Each week, ahead of the coming shoot days with VFX.*

Write what VFX needs on each coming day, copy the email into your own mail, and mark it as sent.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

On the Home tab, press Sync Now, so the coming days, scenes and sets are today's.

✓ When it has worked: The planner's top line says when ShotGrid was last synced.

### 2. Open the planner and choose the days

The Plan tab shows how many coming days have VFX, and how many are planned and sent. The planner lists them a week at a time.

1. Choose how far ahead to look at the top of the list. Untick Only days with VFX to see every day.
2. Click a day. A day with two units has a row for each, so each unit can have its own email.
3. Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) to put several days in one email, or click a week's name to take the whole week.

**Press:** `Open the planner`

✓ When it has worked: The chosen day's plan opens in the middle.

### 3. Write the plan

Everything is kept as you type. Only days with something written go into the email.

1. Under Scenes the email names, the scenes with VFX are already picked. Click a scene to add it or take it off.
2. Write the VFX work expected in a line or two: what, not how.
3. Choose a department and press Add a department, then write what you're asking of them or assuming they've arranged.
4. Tick the lidar, scanning and reference work, adding a detail if needed.
5. What ShotGrid says about this day shows the VFX notes, set notes and breakdown lines ShotGrid already holds.

✓ When it has worked: The day shows Draft in the list.

### 4. Copy the email

The email panel shows the subject, To, Cc and a preview. The app never sends anything: you paste it into a new message in your own email.

1. Type over Forward look at to say it your own way, if you like.
2. Copy the subject, To and Cc with the copy button beside each, and paste them into a new message.
3. Press Copy email and paste it into the body.
4. If a warning says nobody receives a department's asks, choose someone on Who gets what.

**Press:** `Copy email`

✓ When it has worked: A line under the buttons says it was copied.

### 5. Save it as a PDF, if wanted

The same plan as a PDF, to attach or send on its own. It shows names, never email addresses.

**Press:** `Save as PDF`

✓ When it has worked: The PDF opens once it is saved.

### 6. Mark it as sent

Once you've sent the email from your own mail, mark it here and confirm. If a plan changes afterwards it shows Changed since sent, so you know to send an update.

**Press:** `Mark as sent`

✓ When it has worked: The day shows Sent and today's date.


## Words you'll see

- **Crew list** — The production office's PDF of everyone on the crew, by department. The app keeps names, jobs and emails, never phone numbers.
- **Forward look** — The email telling departments what VFX needs on the coming shoot days.
- **Who gets what** — Who receives each department's asks. To start with, that's the department's head from the crew list.
- **Always copied in** — People on Cc of every email, whichever departments are asked.
- **Lidar** — A laser scan of a set, so VFX can rebuild it accurately.
- **Changed since sent** — A plan edited after it was marked as sent. Send an update.
