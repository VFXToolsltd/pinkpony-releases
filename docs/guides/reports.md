# Reports: how to

The Reports tab makes the show's reports from the last update from ShotGrid: cost workbooks and PDFs, hot costs, vendors and shot lists. Two buttons at the top right of the window open the reports for producers and executives, and Report Studio for designing your own.

Each job below lists what to press, in order, and what you'll see when it has worked.

**Jobs on this tab**

- [Weekly reports](#reports-weekly) — Once a week, or whenever the cost figures are needed.
- [Hot costs](#reports-hot-costs) — When shots are flagged for cost: the Home tab shows a Hot Costs banner.
- [Vendors, bids and turnover](#reports-vendors) — When someone asks what has been awarded, what is out for bid, or what has been handed over.
- [Shot lists for the team](#reports-shots) — Whenever the coordinators need a list of shots. No money is shown.
- [Series Overview workbook](#reports-series) — When someone needs the whole series in one spreadsheet.
- [Check the cost to date](#reports-ctd) — Before reports go out, on a show that tracks each shot's cost to date.
- [Update the Google Sheet](#reports-sheet) — When the project's Google Sheet of shots needs bringing up to date.
- [Reports for producers and executives](#reports-stakeholder) — When producers or executives want an update they can read without the spreadsheets.
- [Design your own report](#reports-studio) — When none of the ready-made reports shows what someone needs.
- [Have an AI assistant draft a design](#reports-studio-ai) — When you'd rather describe a report than build it.

## Weekly reports

*When: Once a week, or whenever the cost figures are needed.*

The Excel workbooks and budget PDFs, from today's figures in ShotGrid.

### 1. Get the shot pictures

Saves to this computer any shot picture the reports don't have yet, so every shot shows its picture.

**Press:** `Download / Refresh Thumbs`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shot pictures were saved.

### 2. Make the weekly reports

Gets the latest from ShotGrid itself, then makes an Excel workbook per episode, the Budget Overview PDF, a Financial Comparison PDF per episode and a check file of every shot's cost. Each of the first three also has its own button under Financial Reports.

1. The Financial Comparison shows each shot's cost and status now against the previous update from ShotGrid, whoever made it.
2. For the whole set at once (the weekly reports plus the shot and hot cost PDFs, turnover, vendor award, bidding and package reports), press Run All Reports instead. It takes a few minutes.

**Press:** `Weekly Reports (All)`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears.

### 3. Open the reports

Opens the reports folder. Today's reports are in the folder with today's date.

**Press:** `Open Reports Folder`

## Hot costs

*When: When shots are flagged for cost: the Home tab shows a Hot Costs banner.*

The shots flagged for cost, per episode and all together.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So the flags and costs are today's.

**Press:** `Sync All ShotGrid Data`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shots were brought in.

### 2. A hot cost PDF per episode

Each episode's flagged shots, with their picture, cost, vendor and cost comment.

**Press:** `Hot Cost Reports`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears.

### 3. One summary of them all

Every flagged shot in the project, in one PDF.

**Press:** `Master Hot Cost Summary`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears. The PDFs are in today's folder inside the reports folder.

*When: When someone asks what has been awarded, what is out for bid, or what has been handed over.*

Vendor awards, bidding packages, turnover costs and the package cost statement.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So the vendors, statuses and costs are today's.

**Press:** `Sync All ShotGrid Data`

Each vendor's shots and total, with the grand total, as a PDF.

**Press:** `Vendor Award Summary`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears.

### 3. What is out for bid

Per episode, the shots out for bid, with their bidding brief, bidding vendors and assumptions.

**Press:** `Bidding Package PDF`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears.

### 4. What has been turned over

Per episode, the shots and assets handed to vendors, with status, vendor and cost, and the episode's costs to date.

**Press:** `Turnover & Costs to Date`

✓ When it has worked: A Done message appears.

The VFX costs as one budget account, a line per episode for shots and assets, actual costs only. Saved as a spreadsheet and a PDF.

**Press:** `VFX Package (Movie Magic)`

✓ When it has worked: A message says the statement was saved as a spreadsheet and a PDF.

## Series Overview workbook

*When: When someone needs the whole series in one spreadsheet.*

One Excel workbook: a tab per episode, plus overheads, assets and a summary, with the columns you choose.

### 1. Choose the columns (once)

Tick the columns to include and put them in order. The project remembers them.

1. Press Choose Columns…
2. Tick the columns you want. Select All and Select None tick or clear them all.
3. Drag a row, or use Move Up and Move Down, to set the order.
4. Press Save. Reset to Default goes back to the standard columns.

**Press:** `Choose Columns…`

### 2. Make the workbook

Makes the workbook from the last update from ShotGrid.

**Press:** `Series Overview (Excel)`

✓ When it has worked: A message gives the file's name. It's in today's folder inside the reports folder.

## Check the cost to date

*When: Before reports go out, on a show that tracks each shot's cost to date.*

Find finished shots whose cost to date doesn't match their full cost, and correct them.

### 1. Switch it on (once)

In Settings (the gear button at the top right), on the Budget tab, tick Include Cost to Date (CTD) in financial reports, then press Save Changes.

**Press:** `Settings`

### 2. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So every shot's cost and cost to date are today's.

**Press:** `Sync All ShotGrid Data`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shots were brought in.

### 3. Run the check

Lists the finished shots whose cost to date doesn't match their full cost: a PDF to read and a spreadsheet to correct.

**Press:** `CTD Error Check (CSV + PDF)`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shots were flagged, or that every finished shot matches.

### 4. Correct them in ShotGrid

Correct the cost to date in the spreadsheet, then import it into ShotGrid. It carries each shot's ID, so the right shots are updated.

1. In ShotGrid, open the Shots page.
2. Menu (☰) → Import → choose the corrected spreadsheet.
3. Make sure it says it will UPDATE existing shots, then import.

## Reports for producers and executives

*When: When producers or executives want an update they can read without the spreadsheets.*

Plain-language pages with charts, which open in any web browser.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So the figures are today's.

**Press:** `Sync All ShotGrid Data`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shots were brought in.

### 2. Get the shot pictures

The contact sheet and hot cost pages show each shot's picture.

**Press:** `Download / Refresh Thumbs`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many shot pictures were saved.

### 3. Open the list of reports

Press the bookshelf button at the top right of the window, beside Settings. Some projects show only some of the reports.

**Press:** `Stakeholder Reports`

### 4. Make the report you need

Press a report's button. It's made and opens in your web browser.

1. Weekly Dashboard: the week at a glance. It is the same report as Weekly Overview on the Home tab.
2. Episode Budget Gauges: each episode's spend against its budget.
3. Shot Completion Burn-Down: how many shots have been finalled, week by week.
4. Vendor Scorecard: each vendor's shots and costs, by status.
5. Hot Cost Alert Summary: the shots flagged for cost, largest first.
6. Episode Progress Contact Sheet: every shot's picture and status, by episode.
7. Notes & Feedback Digest: the last week's notes, from the last work brief made on the Brief tab.

✓ When it has worked: A message says your report is ready and open.

### 5. Or make them all

Makes every report on the list and opens the folder they're saved in.

**Press:** `Generate ALL Reports`

✓ When it has worked: A message says how many reports were made.

## Design your own report

*When: When none of the ready-made reports shows what someone needs.*

Build a report section by section in Report Studio. No code is needed.

### 1. Open Report Studio

Press the pencil button at the top right of the window, beside the bookshelf. It lists the stored designs.

**Press:** `Report Studio`

### 2. Try a starter first

Choose a design in the list ([starter] ones come with the app, [yours] are your own) and press Render Selected to see it in your browser.

**Press:** `Render Selected`

✓ When it has worked: The report opens in your browser.

### 3. Start a design

Opens the builder: give the design a name and a title, and choose under Data what the report is about. To change an existing design instead, choose it and press Edit Selected…; a starter is never changed, and your version is saved beside it.

**Press:** `New Design…`

### 4. Add the sections

Choose a kind (stat row, table, chart, heading, text, note, divider or page break) and press Add Section, then fill in its settings in the middle.

1. For a figure or a column, click the box first, then double-click the field in Available data on the right.
2. Use ↑ and ↓ to change the order, and Remove to delete a section.
3. If Available data is empty, there is no data yet. Get the latest from ShotGrid, then open the builder again.

**Press:** `Add Section`

### 5. Preview it

Opens the report as it stands in your browser. Nothing is saved.

**Press:** `Preview`

### 6. Save the design

Checks the design can be drawn, then saves it. If something's wrong it names the section, and nothing is saved.

**Press:** `Save`

✓ When it has worked: A message says the design is saved and ready to use in Report Studio.

### 7. Make the report

Back in Report Studio, choose your design and press Render Selected.

**Press:** `Render Selected`

✓ When it has worked: The report opens in your browser.


<a id="reports-studio-ai"></a>

## Have an AI assistant draft a design

*When: When you'd rather describe a report than build it.*

Give an AI assistant a brief, then bring its design back in. The app itself never contacts one.

### 1. Open Report Studio

Press the pencil button at the top right of the window.

**Press:** `Report Studio`

### 2. Write the brief

Choose under Data what the report should show (or New report), then export the brief. It opens, is saved in the reports folder, and holds no figures from the show.

**Press:** `Export Design Brief`

✓ When it has worked: A message says the design brief is saved and open.

### 3. Ask the assistant

Give the brief to your AI assistant with what the report should show, and save its answer as a .json or .html file.

### 4. Bring the design in

Choose the file you saved.

**Press:** `Import Design (.json / .html)…`

✓ When it has worked: A message says the design is imported and ready to use.

### 5. Make the report

Choose the design in the list and press Render Selected.

**Press:** `Render Selected`

✓ When it has worked: The report opens in your browser.


## Words you'll see

- **Reports folder** — Where reports are saved: the Export Dir set in Settings, or a folder on your Desktop. Most go in a folder dated the day they were made.
- **Thumbnails** — The small shot pictures the reports show.
- **Hot cost** — A shot flagged in ShotGrid because its cost needs watching.
- **Turnover** — Handing a shot to a vendor to work on.
- **Cost to date (CTD)** — What has been spent on a shot so far. A finished shot's should equal its full cost.
- **Design** — A report layout kept in Report Studio. Starters come with the app; yours are saved beside them.
