# Finance: how to

The Finance tab keeps the show's budget, what has been spent, and what is left. It builds the cost tracker spreadsheet and sends the totals to ShotGrid.

Open the Finance tab. At the top it asks What are you doing? — choose the job below and it walks you through the same steps, ticking each one off as it's done.

**Jobs on this tab**

- [Weekly update](#finance-weekly) — Once a week, before the cost report goes out.
- [Budget reissued](#finance-reissue) — When the studio sends a new locked budget PDF.
- [New accounts report](#finance-bible) — When the accounts team sends a new spending report (the 'bible').
- [Add paperwork](#finance-paperwork) — When a PO, invoice or quote arrives as a PDF or Excel file.
- [First-time setup](#finance-setup) — Once per show, before anything else on this tab.

## Weekly update

*When: Once a week, before the cost report goes out.*

Bring the numbers up to date, check them, and send them to ShotGrid.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

Downloads the newest POs, invoices and shots, so everything after this works from today's numbers.

**Press:** `Get the latest`

✓ When it has worked: The status line says when it last updated.

### 2. Update the tracker

Copies what the POs and invoices say into the cost tracker spreadsheet. Anything typed by hand is kept.

**Press:** `Update the tracker`

✓ When it has worked: A green message says how much is committed and spent.

### 3. Add this week's accounts report

The accounts team's spending report (the 'bible') shows what has really been paid. Skip this if no new one arrived this week.

1. Save the accounts team's spreadsheet somewhere you can find it.
2. Press Add the accounts report and choose that file.
3. If it asks you to review lines, open Review, and for each line choose Approve (it's right) or Reject (it's wrong — say why).
4. Save, then press Add the accounts report again.

**Press:** `Add the accounts report`

*You can skip this step when it doesn't apply — press “Skip this step”.*

✓ When it has worked: The accounts report card shows the file name and today's date.

### 4. Look over the tracker

Open the cost tracker and check nothing looks wrong — a big jump, a negative 'left' figure, an account you don't recognise.

**Press:** `Open the tracker`

*Done outside the app — then press “I've done this”.*

### 5. Send the numbers to ShotGrid

Shows you exactly what will change first. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

**Press:** `Preview and send`

✓ When it has worked: A message confirms how many accounts were updated.

### 6. Make the studio report

Creates the spreadsheet that goes to the studio: US dollars, dated, no internal notes.

**Press:** `Make the studio report`

✓ When it has worked: The report opens, and is saved in the Studio\_Exports folder.

## Budget reissued

*When: When the studio sends a new locked budget PDF.*

Load the new budget, check what moved, and send it on.

### 1. Load the new budget and approve it

Choose the new budget PDF. The app shows every account that went up or down and asks you for a reason before anything changes.

1. Press Load the new budget and choose the PDF the studio sent.
2. Read the list of accounts that changed.
3. Type the reason (for example 'V11 — added episode 7 crowd work').
4. Press Accept.
5. Two warnings follow, because the old budget is locked: say Yes to the first, then type the old budget's name exactly as shown in the second.

**Press:** `Load the new budget`

✓ When it has worked: The budget card shows the new version and today's date.

### 2. Check the tracker

The studio's changes are added as uplifts in the tracker's Adjustments tab. Make sure they look right.

**Press:** `Open the tracker`

*Done outside the app — then press “I've done this”.*

### 3. Send the new figures to ShotGrid

Shows you exactly what will change first. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

**Press:** `Preview and send`

✓ When it has worked: A message confirms how many accounts were updated.

## New accounts report

*When: When the accounts team sends a new spending report (the 'bible').*

Add it, answer anything that doesn't match, and send the result on.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So the report is checked against today's POs.

**Press:** `Get the latest`

### 2. Add the accounts report

Choose the spreadsheet. Lines that don't match our own POs are shown to you before anything is added.

1. Press Add the accounts report and choose the file.
2. If it asks you to review lines, open Review.
3. For each line waiting on you choose Approve (it's right) or Reject (it's wrong — type why). Notes are kept.
4. Save, then press Add the accounts report again and say Yes.

**Press:** `Add the accounts report`

✓ When it has worked: The accounts report card shows the file name and today's date.

### 3. Send the numbers to ShotGrid

Shows you exactly what will change first. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

**Press:** `Preview and send`

✓ When it has worked: A message confirms how many accounts were updated.

## Add paperwork

*When: When a PO, invoice or quote arrives as a PDF or Excel file.*

Put the document into ShotGrid — no need to create the PO or invoice first.

### 1. Get the latest from ShotGrid

So the vendors, accounts and existing POs you choose from are today's.

**Press:** `Get the latest`

### 2. Add the document and check it

Choose the file. The app reads the number, total, vendor and account and shows you the line of the page each came from. Nothing is sent until you say so.

1. Press Add a PO, invoice or quote, then Add documents… and choose the file (or drag it onto the window).
2. Check each value against the page shown beside it. Wrong? Pick another from the list or type it.
3. Choose the vendor and, for a PO or invoice, the account. For an invoice, choose the PO it bills against.
4. The total is always BEFORE VAT — VAT is the accounts team's. Tick 'I have checked the total (before VAT) against the document'.
5. Anything in red must be fixed. Anything with a tick box is a question — tick it only if it is right.
6. Press Send to ShotGrid, then Yes.

**Press:** `Add a PO, invoice or quote`

✓ When it has worked: The document's row says 'sent as' with its number.

### 3. Update the tracker

Get the latest from ShotGrid first, then copy the new figures into the cost tracker. Skip this if more paperwork is still to come today.

**Press:** `Update the tracker`

*You can skip this step when it doesn't apply — press “Skip this step”.*

✓ When it has worked: A green message says how much is committed and spent.

## First-time setup

*When: Once per show, before anything else on this tab.*

Point the app at the show's finance folder and load the budget.

### 1. Choose the show's finance folder

In Settings, set Finance Dir to the folder where the show keeps its budgets and cost reports.

**Press:** `Open Settings`

✓ When it has worked: Finance Dir is filled in on the Project tab of Settings.

### 2. Load the locked budget

Choose the studio's locked budget PDF. This becomes the budget everything else is measured against.

**Press:** `Load the budget`

✓ When it has worked: The budget card shows the budget version.

### 3. Send the budget to ShotGrid

Shows you exactly what will change first. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

**Press:** `Preview and send`


## Words you'll see

- **ShotGrid** — The show's online database. The app reads from it and sends totals to it.
- **Budget** — The studio's locked budget PDF. Everything is measured against it.
- **Cost tracker** — The spreadsheet the app keeps for the show: budget, spent, committed and what is left, per account.
- **Accounts report (the 'bible')** — The accounts team's spreadsheet of everything actually paid. It is more complete than our own POs.
- **Committed** — Money promised on a purchase order but not paid yet.
- **Actual** — Money already paid.
- **Uplift** — Extra budget the studio has approved for an account.
- **Preview** — Shows what would change. Nothing is sent or saved.
